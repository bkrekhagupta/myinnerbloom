# Myinnerbloom

Create a landing Page for my Inner Bloom coaching business.

Follow the brief in the attached document and the accepted landing page structure:
1. Navigation: Inner Bloom logo, About, Approach, Offerings, and 'Book a Call' CTA.
2. Hero Section (Non-figurative abstract sanctuary): No human figures, photos, silhouettes, or religious symbols. Abstract layered luminous lotus-inspired petal geometry opening outward, using cosmic purple (#3B1E5D), royal violet (#6D3FA0), soft lavender (#C8B6E2), blush pink (#F2C6D4), warm ivory (#FFF9F5), and muted gold (#C6A15B). Eyebrow: 'INNER BLOOM · TRANSFORMATIONAL MINDSET COACHING', Headline: 'Come Home to Yourself', Supporting headline: 'Understand the stories that shape you. Discover the freedom to write a new one.', Dual CTAs: 'Book a Free Discovery Call' and 'Explore Your Inner Bloom', with reassurance microcopy.
3. Feel Seen (Pattern Reflection): Helping visitors recognise internal beliefs, emotional patterns, and inner conflicts.
4. The Approach (Mindset Reframing): Explaining how familiar stories and automatic responses shape choices, and how Inner Bloom guides conscious transformation.
5. Coaching Pathways: Clear presentation of 1:1 individual coaching and small-group coaching options.
6. Meet Your Guide: Introducing Rekha Devi Gupta, her grounded, reflective, structured approach.
7. Discovery Call & Booking: Interactive booking modal / scheduling inquiry with reassurance microcopy.
8. Calming, responsive design with high accessibility, generous negative space, and refined typography.
